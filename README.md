### Where WordFarm started
WordFarm began in college as [cjk-trainer](https://github.com/mruiz42/cjk-trainer) (2020), a flashcard app for studying vocabulary. I used it, and kept picturing what it could become. WordFarm is me finishing that side project: rebuilt and launched as a full-stack app.
